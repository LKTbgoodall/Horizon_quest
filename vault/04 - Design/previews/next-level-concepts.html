Horizon Quests: next-level concepts

Six directions for a quest book that could not exist in a classic Java mod screen. Everything is animated; drag on a picture to turn it. Item icons are coloured stand-ins: the game draws the real ones. Each card says which Horizon drawing helper it relies on and how sure it is.

AThe 3D board

The chapter becomes a table seen in perspective. Quests are pedestals that rise when done; lines run on the floor; rewards waiting shine as a pillar. Drag to orbit, hover a quest.

native_quad: available todayflat-shaded faces, real item icons on top

BThe constellation

Quests are stars in space, each chapter a constellation. Done quests burn bright, locked ones are faint. Other chapters float far away: clicking one flies the camera there.

native_quad + native_sprite: available todayglow from a texture

CThe block island

Each chapter is a small world made of real Minecraft blocks, turning slowly. Quests are places on it: the forest, the mine, the portal on the mountain. The pack maker builds the island in the editor.

native_draw_scene: in the engine, limits to measurereal block models and textures

DA diorama in every quest

The book stays as it is, but a quest window can show a turning 3D scene of what to build or find: the portal frame, a starter base, an ore vein. Like Create's Ponder, inside a quest.

native_draw_scene: in the engine, to try in game

EA book that feels alive

Finishing a quest is an event: the node bursts, a wave runs down the lines and the next quests unlock one after the other, with sound. Works with the flat book or with A / B.

native_quad + native_sprite + native_play_local_sound: available today

FQuests in the world

The tracked quest leaves the book: a beam and a floating label show where to go, through walls, with the distance. A finished quest fires a beam at the player's feet.

native_world_primitives: in the engine, not numbered yetneeds quests with a place
