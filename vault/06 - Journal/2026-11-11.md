## Dependency lines, v0.6.1
- Bastien (screenshots of FTB Quests): lines "globally the same as FTB Quests", with colours by where a line comes from and what it opens when the pointer is on a quest, so you don't get lost. Reimplemented, no FTB code or asset.
- **`hq_canvas::chevrons(a, b, width, spacing)`**: chevrons along a line pointing towards `b`, each two quads (arms meeting at the tip, 0.3 x width before and behind its point, 0.32 x width to each side, arms 0.24 x width thick), one every `spacing`, at most 64 a line. Tested.
- **`hq_book`**: lines are bands 5 canvas units thick (`LINE_WIDTH`, at least 3 pixels) with chevrons every 9 units (`CHEVRON_STEP`, at least 8 pixels) in a darker shade (`darker`: 3/5 of each channel). The quest in focus is the one under the pointer, else the selected one: lines into it (what it needs) are cyan `LINE_NEEDS`, lines out of it (what it opens) yellow `LINE_OPENS`, drawn after the others. Otherwise a line is green when its dependency is completed, else a muted mauve `LINE` (lighter than the old grey, which hardly showed on the dark background).
- Checked with `cargo run --example book_preview` opened in the built-in browser: the hovered "Timber" shows a cyan line from "Welcome" and a yellow one to the next quest, chevrons visible.

## The quest window, v0.7.0
- Bastien (FTB Quests screenshot): no more panel on the right; a small window when a quest is clicked, with its description, the items to hand in, the rewards...
- **`hq_book`**: the side panel is gone (`draw_panel`, `PanelRow`, `ICON_ROW` removed; `select` no longer moves the canvas). `popup_layout(s, statuses, n, canvas)` gives the window's rect (300 wide at most, as tall as its content up to 200, centred over the canvas) and its content: `task_icons`, `reward_icons` (`Icon { item, count, action, tip, veil }`), the columns' height (as many rows of 20-pixel icon cells as the longer column needs) and `popup_lines` (state with optional / repeatable / done N times, "Needs:" with each dependency, subtitle, description). `draw_popup`: frame, title bar (quest icon, centred title, Track / Untrack, x), two columns headed "Tasks" (or "Tasks, in order", in blue) and "Rewards" (gold) drawn by `draw_icons` (centred rows, hover shade under the icon, count at the bottom right, a green veil over a task done, a dark one over a waiting task or a claimed reward, "+N" past 24, "None"), then the text, scrolled by the wheel (`panel_scroll`, `Scene.popup`).
- **Clicks**: a canvas-wide `Deselect` zone under every other zone (an empty spot closes the window, another node opens its own), `Action::Stay` (renamed from `KeepRecipe`) on the window. Task icon: checkmark → `Check`, open hand-in → `Submit`, other item or craft task → `ShowRecipe`. Reward icon: claimable → `Claim`; an unclaimed choice reward shows each entry, a click `Choose`s it; claimed or not yet claimable item → `ShowRecipe`. Tooltips say what a click does ("- click to tick it", "- click to hand in", "- click to claim", "- click to take this one", "- click for its recipes"); a random reward's lists its entries.
- **Icons** (`task_item`, `reward_item`): the item itself; a writable book for a checkmark, `<entity>_spawn_egg` for a kill, an experience bottle for levels and XP (count: points, or `NL`), netherrack / end stone / grass block for a dimension, a compass for a place, a knowledge book for an advancement, a command block, paper for a toast, a chest for a table. A `#tag` is drawn as the first item of the game's list that has the tag: `hq_client_glue` keeps `tag_icons`, fills it on the tick from `native_recipes().items` for the tags painting met (`tags_wanted`), `minecraft:barrier` when none has it.
- The example quest texts said "Tick the box below": now "Click the book under Tasks".
- Checked in the `book_preview` pictures. Version 0.7.0. **171 unit + 4 + 1 + 2 tests pass** (eight book tests rewritten for the window); `check-glue.ps1`: status ok, no warning from the mod.

## Marks, counts, item tooltips, pin: v0.7.1
- Bastien (FTB screenshots): a check on what is done, a "!" on a reward to take, a real Minecraft item tooltip on an item (not a description of the quest), prettier counts like an inventory's, and a pin pictogram instead of "Track".
- **`hq_book`**: `Icon { item, count, action, tip: Vec<(String, u32)>, veil, mark }`; `Mark::Check` (a task done, a reward claimed) and `Mark::Alert` (a reward to claim, each entry of a claimable choice) drawn at the icon's top-right corner from pixel maps (`pixel_art` merges runs of a colour into one fill): a green check with a dark edge, a red disc with a white "!". The green veil over a done task is gone; the dark veil stays on a waiting task.
- **Counts**: what the task asks (its goal, not the progress), or the reward's count, drawn like an inventory's: right-aligned so it ends one pixel past the icon, 9 pixels down.
- **Tooltips**: `draw_tooltip` takes lines. An item's (`item_tip`): its name (from `BookView.item_names`, which the client glue fills once per world from `native_recipes().items`, else made from its id; a tag is "Any <tag>"), its mod in Minecraft's blue (`Minecraft` for `minecraft:`), then the task's own words, "Progress: 3/8" or "Done", "One of these, your choice", "Team reward...", a random reward's entries, "Claimed", then the click in yellow ("Click to tick it", "Click to hand in", "Click to claim", "Click to take this one", "Click to view recipe(s)"). A quest node keeps its title alone.
- **Pin**: a 7 x 9 pixel push pin in the window's title bar (grey, gold while tracked, white under the pointer) replaces the Track / Untrack button; its tooltip says "Pin: show it while you play" / "Unpin: ...".
- Example quest text: "press Submit" became "click the rotten flesh under Tasks".
- Version 0.7.1. 171 unit + 4 + 1 + 2 tests pass (tooltip tests compare whole lines); `check-glue.ps1`: status ok.

- Bastien: he does not like the checks (too thin and small in game); levels should show "2", not "2L" (the tooltip already says "2 levels"); a choice reward must show some other way: nobody understands that one has to choose.

- **Levels**: `reward_item` gives the number alone.
- **Choice**: one icon (a chest) per choice reward, marked with a gold "?" (`Mark::Choice`, also outlined), or the red "!" once the quest is done; its tooltip lists the entries and says "Click to see the choices" / "Click to choose". `Action::OpenChoice(reward)` sets `BookView.choosing`; `draw_choice` draws a 200-wide window over the book: "Choose one reward", "Finish the quest, then pick one." or "Click the one you want.", then each entry (icon, count, name; hover shade and an item tooltip once claimable). A click on an entry sends the claim with that choice and closes the window; a click outside (`CloseChoice`) or Escape (client glue) closes it. Drag and wheel do nothing under it.
