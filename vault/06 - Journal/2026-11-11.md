## How to publish the content half (ADR-014)
- Bastien: "up to you to find how we publish the content part".
- At our pin (`fdc87ae`) the docs said the registry publishes native mods as code-only `.hnmod` files until launchers read native `.hmod`s. Horizon `main` (read 2026-11-11: `docs/api/mod-files.md`, `docs/native-mods.md`, horizon-mods' README) has moved on: **native registry entries may name a `.hmod` "optionally with content"**; the launcher installs it, validates the content and snapshots it under the build's `native-content/<id>/`, and the game layers it through its content loader. Older launchers need a code-only `.hnmod`.
- Our `build/horizon_quests.hmod` already follows the rules (59 entries; top level only `assets/`, `horizon-mod.toml`, `horizon-native.json`, `mixins/`; the same id in both manifests; content paths valid).

- **Found on the way**: the registry compiles native mods **on the newest Nightly** (horizon-mods `native-compat.yml`) and marks versions `broken_on` a commit where they no longer apply. The launcher here is on `nightly-2026-11-08-fdc87ae`; Horizon `main` is newer. So 1.0.0 needs a re-pin first: Bastien updates the launcher to the newest Nightly, then the glue is checked and the pins updated. Also: the registry's own check, `cargo horizon-mod check <file>`.
