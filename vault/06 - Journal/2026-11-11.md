## Assets, v0.6.0
- Bastien: "go" for the last phase 3 step.
- API read in the pin: `native_sprite(batch, name, src, dst) -> bool` (API 19) draws a part of a GUI sprite; the HUD atlas packs `assets/<ns>/textures/gui/**` of every non-minecraft namespace as `<ns>:gui/<path>` (`hud.rs`); **no tint**, so a frame per state. `native_sprite_size` exists too. The registry wants a PNG icon of at most 256 KiB.
- **`tools/make-node-textures.py`** (new, zlib PNG): `mod/content/assets/horizon_quests/textures/gui/nodes.png`, 160 x 160: rows square, circle, diamond, hexagon, octagon; columns locked, available, in progress, rewards waiting, done (`state_look`'s colours). Each 32-pixel cell: a 1-pixel dark outline, a 2.5-pixel ring in the state's colour (lighter on the top-left half, darker on the bottom-right), a 1-pixel inner shadow, the dark face. Corners follow `shape_points`, so the frame matches the fallback polygons. Preview in `build/node_textures_preview.png`.
- **`hq_book`**: `Draw::Sprite { name, src, dst, fallback }`; `NODE_SPRITE`, `node_cell(shape, state)`; a node is one sprite over its box, the two polygons as its fallback (the client glue paints the fallback when `native_sprite` returns false). The gold selection outline stays a polygon under it. `examples/book_preview.rs` flattens sprites to their fallback (no textures in the SVG).
- **Icon**: `tools/make-book-texture.py` also writes `mod/icon.png`, the Quest Book 16 times larger (256 x 256, 1.3 KB). Not packed in the mod: it is for the registry form.
- Tests: `nodes_are_drawn_from_the_frame_sheet_with_polygons_to_fall_back_on` (book), `the_node_frames_are_where_the_book_draws_them_from` and `the_registry_icon_is_a_small_square_png` (content). **168 unit + 4 + 1 + 2 pass** with `-D warnings`; `check-glue.ps1`: status ok, no warning from the mod.

## Dependency lines, v0.6.1
- Bastien (screenshots of FTB Quests): lines "globally the same as FTB Quests", with colours by where a line comes from and what it opens when the pointer is on a quest, so you don't get lost. Reimplemented, no FTB code or asset.
- **`hq_canvas::chevrons(a, b, width, spacing)`**: chevrons along a line pointing towards `b`, each two quads (arms meeting at the tip, 0.3 x width before and behind its point, 0.32 x width to each side, arms 0.24 x width thick), one every `spacing`, at most 64 a line. Tested.
- **`hq_book`**: lines are bands 5 canvas units thick (`LINE_WIDTH`, at least 3 pixels) with chevrons every 9 units (`CHEVRON_STEP`, at least 8 pixels) in a darker shade (`darker`: 3/5 of each channel). The quest in focus is the one under the pointer, else the selected one: lines into it (what it needs) are cyan `LINE_NEEDS`, lines out of it (what it opens) yellow `LINE_OPENS`, drawn after the others. Otherwise a line is green when its dependency is completed, else a muted mauve `LINE` (lighter than the old grey, which hardly showed on the dark background).
- Checked with `cargo run --example book_preview` opened in the built-in browser: the hovered "Timber" shows a cyan line from "Welcome" and a yellow one to the next quest, chevrons visible.
- Version 0.6.1. **169 unit + 4 + 1 + 2 tests pass**; `check-glue.ps1`: status ok.
